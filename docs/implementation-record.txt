COMMERCE LENS — FINAL IMPLEMENTATION RECORD
14 November 2026

Implemented in Power BI Service, not a static dashboard mock-up.
The report and semantic model were saved. The saved report was reloaded and
tested in reading mode. Its source is the public UCI Online Retail II snapshot.

VISUAL CHANGES
- 1600 x 1100 single-page overview with a slate background, Arial typography,
  teal KPI accents and copper comparison series.
- Six KPI cards, including customer-ID sales coverage. Order/buyer counts
  are exact integers rather than rounded thousands.
- Three readable dropdown controls: year, month coverage, market.
- Year is single-select. The default is 2011, complete months, all markets.
- Country map with verified US/Czech name aliases and logarithmic colour
  intensity. Tooltip values remain actual money and order counts.
- Map coverage is explicitly quantified; unmatched sales remain in totals.
- Top-10 product heatmap in GBP thousands. Months are chronological;
  products are ranked by total net sales. Both 11- and 12-month views fit.
- Monthly current/prior-year sales lines, dashed prior year, with a dynamic
  like-for-like comparison caption. Incomplete full-year coverage is labelled.
- Credit values and credit/gross rates are shown on separate currency and
  percentage axes in one combination chart.
- Top-eight international market filter, excluding the UK from the ranking.
  The title changes when no international sales are selected.
- Buyer frequency uses the explicit BandOrder column, ascending order,
  exact labels, and a repeat-purchase subtitle.
- Public-source attribution and accounting/coverage limitations remain visible.

MODEL CHANGES
- Added 41 namespaced CL measures and three calculated columns.
- Preserved existing measure expressions, relationships and source queries
  used by the other report sharing this model.
- Set PurchaseBand[Band] to sort by BandOrder.
- Added Date[CL Month], sorted by MonthNumber, without changing the existing
  Date[MonthName] column used by other reports.
- Prior-year calculations use the same source-available calendar dates.
- Did not assume the ambiguous source label Korea means South Korea.
- The two small TMDL correction files supersede the matching measures in
  CommerceLens_additive_measures.tmdl.

SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS
- The custom map file-upload control is disabled in this Service editor.
  The report therefore retains its URL-based map boundary dependency,
  including Antarctica. An offline map is not claimed.
- The data is historical, not a live business feed. No profit, traffic,
  conversion, causal return-rate or client-impact claims are made.
- Credits are attributed to credit posting dates, not original sale dates.
- Customer analysis excludes unknown IDs; sales do not exclude them.
- A 2010 December point can have a valid December 2009 comparison even
  though the whole 2010 prior-year comparison is unavailable.
- The browser slicers omit impossible combinations, such as a partial
  month in 2010. A wholly empty-report state was not forced via the UI;
  its calculation guards were tested directly in the native DAX engine.
- Mobile-specific layout, screen-reader tab-order audit and Power BI Desktop
  execution were not tested. This is the desktop/web portfolio overview.
- Claude's design was adapted rather than copied mechanically: dropdown
  controls, a 1100-pixel canvas, native Service reset and consolidated context
  captions were used. Per-KPI YoY reference labels were not added.
- Scheduled-refresh settings and Desktop auto-date/time were not changed.
